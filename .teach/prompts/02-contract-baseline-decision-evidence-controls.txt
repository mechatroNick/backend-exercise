## Abstracted teaching intent

This lower-level visual explains how the implementation avoided silent contract drift.
Authority descends from owner clarification to assessment, accepted ADRs, interpretation,
and plans. Public routes/statuses, identity normalization, one-worker limit, current
statistics meaning, weekly history boundary, and ORM/raw-SQL boundary are locked. Internal
names, fixture design, bounded defaults, and module layout are reversible choices. A
material change must route through owner/ADR review. Evidence is an executable chain:
requirement → SPEC → code/test → verifier → report. A plan cannot be treated as proof.

## Technical style instruction

Pure-white engineering control schematic, clean sans-serif labels, integrated annotations
only. Brick red (#B91C1C) represents locked/public decisions and review stop points;
amber represents reversible local latitude; royal blue (#1D4ED8) durable contracts;
deep purple (#7E22CE) accepted ADR review; muted orange (#EA580C) executable validation;
forest green (#15803D) actual receipt; slate (#475569) planning. Use vertical authority
arrows and horizontal traceability arrows; no hex text, no grids, no detached legend.
