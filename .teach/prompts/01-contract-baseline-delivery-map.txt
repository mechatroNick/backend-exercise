## Source anchors and bounded claims

- `.docs/ASSESSMENT.md` maps requirement ownership; Track 00 controls evidence/decision posture.
- Tracks 00–05 are mandatory core; 06–09 are dependency-gated extensions/hardening.
- Planned work is not treated as executable proof; excluded scope remains local-only.

## Abstracted teaching intent

The visual teaches that the challenge was delivered through a durable control plane, not
by coding features in isolation. The supplied assessment became 43 classified
requirements; solution design, ADRs, and delivery plan made decisions and dependencies
reviewable. Tracks 00–05 establish the mandatory local Bookmarks API. Track 05 is a
quality firewall before the event/current-snapshot extension in Track 06, private weekly
projection extension in Track 07, hardening bonuses in Track 08, and final evidence work
in Track 09. Verification results must feed back to requirements; planned work is not
evidence. Excluded scope stays visible: no public history API, external broker, multiple
web workers, or production infrastructure.
