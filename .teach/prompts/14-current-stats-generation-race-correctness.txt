## Source anchors and bounded claims

mark_dirty atomically increments generation. The worker observes a generation, recomputes and atomically publishes a complete immutable snapshot, then compare-and-deletes only that observed generation. A concurrent newer marker remains pending. Failed SQL/publication retains old complete snapshot and dirty work; reads fall back live.
