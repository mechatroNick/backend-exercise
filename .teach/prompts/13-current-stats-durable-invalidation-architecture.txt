## Source anchors and bounded claims

Material committed create, scalar update, tag update, or delete marks bookmark_stats_window_dirty at (user_id, window_start) in the same transaction, then emits one safe post-commit BookmarkStatsInvalidated wake-up. The bounded queue is not authority; the named bookmark-stats-refresher reconciles durable work and publishes immutable snapshots. Current stats retain canonical parameterized raw-SQL fallback.
