## Source anchors and bounded claims

Track 07 consumes durable dirty markers for UTC Monday-to-Monday half-open event-time windows based on immutable created_at. It keeps working, developed, and state data private; developing points are replaceable and developed points append-only. Current all-current stats and the sole refresher stay unchanged; no public weekly/history operation exists.
